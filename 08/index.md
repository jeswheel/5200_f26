----------------------

An introduction to split-plot designs, terminology, and R anaylsis. A few examples are provided, including examples such as split-plot designs with factorial treatment structure, split-split-plot designs, and split-plot designs with blocking.

Learning Objectives:

- Students will be able to identify split-plot designs and explain how they differ from CRD, RCB, and Nested designs.
- Students will be able to distinguish between whole-plot factors and split-plot factors.
- Students will understand how to formulate mixed-effects models that account for multiple randomizations.
- Students will be able to analyze split-plot data using `lme4` in R.

**Expected reading:** There is no expected readings for this module. However, the material is based on Chapter 16 of Oehlert, G. W. (2020). *A First Course in Design and Analysis of Experiments*, [http://users.stat.umn.edu/~gary/book/fcdae.pdf](http://users.stat.umn.edu/~gary/book/fcdae.pdf), which contains a lot of good information an examples related to split-plot designs.
