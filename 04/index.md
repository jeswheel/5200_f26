This section is intended to introduce experiments with two or more fixed treatment factors. Fixed effects models; main effects versus interactions; characterizing interactions; interpretation of $F$-ratios; and *post hoc* mean comparisons.

**Expected reading:** Chapter 8 of Oehlert, G. W. (2020). *A First Course in Design and Analysis of Experiments*, [http://users.stat.umn.edu/~gary/book/fcdae.pdf](http://users.stat.umn.edu/~gary/book/fcdae.pdf).
