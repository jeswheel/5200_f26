This section is intended to introduce experiments with two or more fixed treatment factors.
Fixed effects models; main effects versus interactions; characterizing interactions; interpretation of $F$-ratios; hierarchical models; and other topics in factorial analysis.

Desired outcomes:

- Students will be able to identify a complete factorial treatment structure.
- Students will develop an intuitive and formal understanding of interaction effects.
- Students will be able to specify and fit additive and non-additive two-way (and higher) ANOVA models.
- Students will be able to construct and interpret main effect and interaction contrasts.
- Students will know how to fit and analyze factorial deata in R.

**Expected reading:** Chapter 8 of Oehlert, G. W. (2020). *A First Course in Design and Analysis of Experiments*, [http://users.stat.umn.edu/~gary/book/fcdae.pdf](http://users.stat.umn.edu/~gary/book/fcdae.pdf).
