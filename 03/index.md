This section is intended to introduce our first type of experimental design: completely randomized designs.
We will also introduce our first form of analysis for a designed experiment: Analysis of Variance.

- Students will understand what a completely randomized design is, and identify strengths and weaknesses.
- Students will be able to define what a statistical model is.

**Expected reading:** Chapter 3 of Oehlert, G. W. (2020). *A First Course in Design and Analysis of Experiments*, [http://users.stat.umn.edu/~gary/book/fcdae.pdf](http://users.stat.umn.edu/~gary/book/fcdae.pdf).
