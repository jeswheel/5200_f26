Nested and nested factorial designs; estimation of variance components by REML.

Learning Objectives

- Students will be able to define the difference between crossed and nested factors.
- Students will be able to write standard linear models for nested factors.
- Students will be able to fit models with nested factors to data using modern statistical software.

**Expected reading:** This materal is loosely based on Chapter 12 of Oehlert, G. W. (2020). *A First Course in Design and Analysis of Experiments*, [http://users.stat.umn.edu/~gary/book/fcdae.pdf](http://users.stat.umn.edu/~gary/book/fcdae.pdf). Students are not expected to read from this chapter, though may find sections 12.1 -- 12.6 useful for a study reference.
